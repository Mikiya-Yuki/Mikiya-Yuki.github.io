abstract: "Methods for classifying and measuring the political thought latent in text (quantitative text analysis) have advanced considerably in political science, but existing work has concentrated on English-language two-party systems such as the United States and the United Kingdom. This paper quantifies long-run change in political thought in China in order to capture the multidimensional structure of political ideas in an Asian language. Using document vectors over the text of Chinese-language Party organs, we trace long-term shifts in the ideology of the Chinese Communist Party. The approach makes visible ideological and policy differences that are easily overlooked but substantively meaningful — changes in collectivization, the expansion of the social domain — and, because it does not presuppose known ideological labels, it is well suited to exploratory data analysis."
