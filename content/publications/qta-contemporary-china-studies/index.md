abstract: "Since the 2010s, quantitative text analysis has spread rapidly across the social sciences, and China studies has rediscovered it as an \"old-but-new\" method. This article reviews (1) its adoption under the data constraints of the Cold War era and its decline after the 1980s, (2) its resurgence since the 2010s and its application to the governing mechanisms of authoritarian regimes, and (3) its future possibilities and challenges. While machine learning and a more diverse research agenda open new ground, responding to data regulation, integrating qualitative insight, and easing the tension with area studies remain open problems."
