abstract: "To document the difficulties faced by China scholars based in Japan, we fielded the survey \"The Experiences of China Scholars in Japan (2025)\" in March 2025. 11.3% of respondents had been told directly by Chinese authorities that their research was politically sensitive; 27.1% had encountered some form of research difficulty, including the unavailability of particular materials or contact between local acquaintances and the authorities; and 21.8% had faced censorship or prior-approval requirements when presenting or publishing research in China. A substantial share also reported that recent detentions have influenced their choice of research topics."
summary: "First results from a March 2025 survey of China scholars based in Japan on political sensitivity, access to materials, and censorship."
