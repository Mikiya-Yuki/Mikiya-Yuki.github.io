abstract: "We present a framework for retrieving content containing disinformation from large text databases. We first build a knowledge base of verified fact-check results drawn from reports published by fact-checking organizations during the 2024 presidential elections in Indonesia and Taiwan. We then integrate this knowledge base into a pre-trained generative model, creating a retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) framework that can identify suspicious items in a given text input. Applying this classification mechanism to a large corpus of social media text filters out items likely to contain disinformation. Given how rare disinformation is within the collected database, the approach substantially improves the efficiency of identifying sample cases."
