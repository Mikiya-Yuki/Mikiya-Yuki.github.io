abstract: "This research note sets out the theory, procedures, and guidelines for the quantitative analysis of Japanese, Chinese, and Korean texts. It applies conventional methods — scaling, topic models, word embeddings, and supervised classification — alongside pretrained models and large language models to real data in all three languages, compares them in terms of validity, reproducibility, interpretability, and the computing resources they require, and proposes research designs that combine methods according to the purpose of the research."
