abstract: "Rapid vaccination is a central element of public health and contributes to a stable economy. Prior work has pointed separately to the spatial distribution of respondents and to the temporal influence of the information field; this study integrates the two, analyzing geographic information and respondent sentiment across Japan while accounting for spatio-temporal variation. Drawing on 25.61 million social media posts covering every prefecture in Japan between 2019 and 2021, together with a survey on COVID-19 vaccination, we show that the speed of vaccine uptake can be predicted by the temporal distribution of the information field and by geospatial mediation and personality (extraversion)."
