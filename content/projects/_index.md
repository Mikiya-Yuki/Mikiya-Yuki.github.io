        ### Forward to the Past: Revival of Traditionalism in Communist China

        *Visiting Fellow project · Harvard-Yenching Institute · 2026–27*

        Since the 1980s the Chinese Communist Party has written Confucian and Legalist ideas, and traditional cultural heritage more broadly, into an official ideology whose Marxist foundations are avowedly anti-traditional. Cultural tradition and revolutionary tradition are now promoted side by side. This project asks how that combination came to be justified, and whether it marks a change of rhetoric or a substantive shift in what the Party believes — a question that bears directly on ideological adaptation and regime longevity under authoritarian rule.
